# 🔥 Bonfire

A tiny, installable PWA for tracking how you watch film & TV franchises.

**Rest at the bonfire, resume your saga.**

- Browse **11 franchises (134 titles)** and pick your watch order: **Release**, **Story/Chronological**, or a **Custom** order you arrange yourself.
- Rate every title with your own 5-star score, alongside reference IMDb & Rotten Tomatoes numbers.
- Track rewatches with a per-title counter.
- Star favourites — they collect in the **Favourites** tab with your ratings and a stats strip.
- **Road to Doomsday** tab — a live countdown to Avengers: Doomsday (18 Dec 2026) with a must-watch / optional catch-up list across the MCU, Fox's X-Men, and (bonus) James Gunn's rebooted DC Universe.
- Works offline and installs to your home screen. All your data stays on your device (localStorage).

## Franchises
Marvel Cinematic Universe · Star Wars · Lord of the Rings & The Hobbit · Harry Potter & Fantastic Beasts · Fast & Furious · The Hunger Games · Mission: Impossible · John Wick · Jurassic Park/World · X-Men (Fox) · DC Universe (Gunn reboot)

## Run it
It's static — no build step. Keep all files in the repo root and enable **GitHub Pages**
(Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root). Then open the URL on your
phone and choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## Files
`index.html` · `manifest.webmanifest` · `sw.js` · `icon-192.png` · `icon-512.png` · `icon-maskable-512.png` · `.nojekyll`

Bundled scores are approximate snapshots — your own ratings are the point.
Post-2026 titles still settling reviews (e.g. Clayface, Lanterns) show "—" until scores firm up.
